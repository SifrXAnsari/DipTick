# Keyboard

The F-key bar shows what F1 to F10 do on the screen you are on: across the top of the window, and on the bottom row of the terminal screen. A key with nothing to do on a screen shows its number, dim. A click on a key does what the key does.

## Chat

- F1 Help, F2 Tabs, F3 Find, F4 Agents: the table, F5 Retry: the last question again, F6 Pane, F7 Attach: a file for your next message (the terminal asks for its path), F8 Stop, F9 Settings, F10 Quit. In the terminal screen, which has one chat at a time, F2 Window opens the window beside it, and the terminal screen goes on working; F6 there makes the arrow keys scroll the chat until F6 or Esc again.
- @name at the start of what you say asks that agent only; see [Agents at one table](agents).
- Enter sends. Shift+Enter or Ctrl+J starts a new line.
- / opens the commands; Tab completes one; the up and down arrows choose. With nothing typed, they bring back what you sent before.
- Esc stops the answer, closes what is open, or leaves a question (an approval: deny).
- Esc three times, quickly, quits and stops everything, the local models included. Held down, Esc counts once.
- F10, Ctrl+Q, /quit, /exit, or exit typed alone quit, and ask first while an answer runs.

## Settings

F1 Help: all about the setting you are on, with the command that does the same; F2 Change; F3 Find a setting in any section; F5 Reload the settings file; F6 Pane; F7 Show file: the settings file, selected in Explorer; F8 Default; F9 Close; F10 Quit.

## Activity (the window)

F1 Help: the page about the audit log; F2 Filter; F3 Find; F4 Range; F5 Refresh; F6 Pane; F7 Export; F8 Verify; F9 Settings; F10 Quit.

## Help

F1 Contents; F2 Back; F3 Search; F4 Pages; F6 Pane; F7 Save as files; F9 Settings; F10 Quit.

## Tabs (the window)

F1 Help: the page about chats and tabs; F2 Close; F3 Find a tab; F6 Pane; F9 Settings; F10 Quit.

## What they're told (the window)

F1 Help: the page about agents at one table; F2 Close; F4 Agents; F5 Refresh; F6 Pane; F9 Settings; F10 Quit.

## Tabs and windows, as in a web browser

- Ctrl+T: a new tab with a new chat. Ctrl+Shift+N: a new private tab. Ctrl+N: a new window.
- Ctrl+W or Ctrl+F4: close the tab; its chat is kept. Ctrl+Shift+T: the last closed tab, back.
- Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab, or Ctrl+PgDn and Ctrl+PgUp: the next and the previous tab.
- Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+8: that tab; Ctrl+9: the last one.
- Ctrl+Shift+PgUp and Ctrl+Shift+PgDn: move the tab left or right.
- Ctrl+click a chat on the left: open it in a new tab. A middle click on a tab closes it.

## The window's other keys

- Ctrl+K: the command list.
- Ctrl+O: pick the model.
- Ctrl+comma: Settings. Ctrl+slash: Help.
- Ctrl+F: find, on the screen you are on.
- Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C: copy the whole chat.
- Ctrl+V with an image or files copied: attaches them to your next message; text pastes as text.
- Shift+Tab: how answers are gated: Ask before each answer, Answers freely, or Dry run.
- Shift+F10, or the menu key: the right-click menu of what has focus.

## The terminal screen's other keys

- PgUp and PgDn scroll the chat.
- Ctrl+Q quits.
- Shift+Enter needs a terminal that reports it; the console host and Windows Terminal do. Ctrl+J always works.
